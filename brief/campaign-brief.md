# Campaign Brief

Fill in every section below. Short and specific beats long and vague. Not sure what good looks like? Open `brief/example-brief.md`. Want help? Pick **brand-strategist** in Chat and it will interview you and fill this in with you.

## Product

What is it, in one sentence a stranger would understand?

## Audience

Who exactly? A role or a life situation, not "everyone".

## Problem they have

What is going wrong in their day that sends them looking for you?

## Promise

The one thing you claim. One sentence.

## Proof

Why should they believe it? Numbers, customers, demos, founder credibility.

## Conversion goal

The ONE action a visitor should take. Pick one and only one.

## Tone

Three words.

## Landing page URL

Your Grona page.

## Competitors

Up to 3 URLs.
