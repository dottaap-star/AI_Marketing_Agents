## The objective

> "A complete campaign, and a live website experience in Grona it points at. Then 1,000 synthetic customers walk your funnel, ad to page to decision. Best conversion wins."

## How you are judged

There is no human panel. An AI simulation scores every team, the same way for everyone.

| Points | What it measures | In plain words |
|---|---|---|
| 50 | Simulated funnel performance | A synthetic audience is built from your own stated ICP. Those customers see your ad, read your copy, land on your page, and decide. The more of them who convert, the more you score. Know your audience and the funnel writes itself. |
| 30 | The agent your team built | Your custom agent is run against a brief you have never seen. It is scored on what it produces on its own. A sharp, specific agent beats a vague one. |
| 20 | Grona execution depth | A live campaign, and a page genuinely shaped by it. A real experience scores, a placeholder does not. |
